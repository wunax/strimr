This product includes the `dca` crate 0.8.0 from the harletty-bridge project
(https://github.com/harletty/harletty-bridge), the DTS Coherent Acoustics, DTS-HD Master Audio
and DTS:X decoder, built from commit a717bae441b116485f0254603dc20a6ede65d8c6, unmodified. The
crate is labelled Apache-2.0, but its sources describe it as a port of FFmpeg's dca decoder
(libavcodec/dca.c, dca_core.c, dca_exss.c, dca_xll.c, dcadsp.c, dcaadpcm.h, and tables generated
from the libavcodec sources), which is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or later. It is treated here as a work based on FFmpeg under that license
(AetherEngine#798). Copyright in those parts belongs to the FFmpeg authors.

The DTS:X reconstruction in src/engine.rs (fold removal before the bed and the extension feeds
are rendered side by side, and the refusal of folds that cannot be resolved) follows
SpatialAudioBridge (https://github.com/kristofferR/SpatialAudioBridge), licensed under the Apache
License, Version 2.0, whose 7.1.4 render harness/verify.sh compares against.

The library also contains, compiled in, the Rust standard library and these crates, each
available under the MIT license or the Apache License, Version 2.0: log, num-complex,
num-integer, num-traits, primal-check, rustfft, strength_reduce, thiserror, transpose.
