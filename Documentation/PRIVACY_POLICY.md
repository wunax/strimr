# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Strimr ("Strimr", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Strimr application on iOS, macOS, or tvOS (the "App"). Strimr is a third-party client for Plex and Jellyfin and is not affiliated with Plex, Inc. or the Jellyfin project. Strimr uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt. Strimr also uses Simkl and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Simkl.

## Summary

- Strimr only collects crash diagnostics to improve app stability (via Sentry).
- Information used by the App remains on your device, except what it exchanges with the services you connect.
- The App communicates with Plex services or your Jellyfin server to function. Plex's handling of data is governed by Plex's privacy policy, while data handled by a Jellyfin server is controlled by that server's operator.
- If you connect Trakt or Simkl, which is optional, what you watch on that profile is sent to that service.

## Information We Collect

We collect only anonymized crash diagnostics through Sentry when the App crashes. This data is used to identify and fix stability issues.

We do not collect or process:

- Personal identifiers (such as name, email address, or account ID)
- Usage analytics (non-crash)
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, or other device data

## Information Stored On Your Device

Strimr stores only the minimum data required for the App to function. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us. Specifically:

- Authentication tokens used to access your Plex account or Jellyfin server through the App
- App settings and preferences
- If you connect Trakt or Simkl: the access tokens for that service, your username on it, your sync settings, and updates waiting to be sent

## How the App Communicates

The App connects directly to Plex services or your Jellyfin server to retrieve and display your media. All requests are made from your device to the selected service or server. Strimr does not operate servers that collect or relay your data.

For details about Plex and Jellyfin data practices, review:

- [Plex Privacy Policy](https://www.plex.tv/about/privacy-legal/)
- [Jellyfin's privacy information](https://jellyfin.org/#privacy-focused)

## Trakt and Simkl

Connecting Trakt or Simkl is optional and is done per profile in Settings > Integrations, by authorizing the App on the service's website. The App never asks for your password on that service.

Once connected, the App keeps your account on that service up to date with what you watch on that profile. It sends, directly from your device:

- The title's identifiers (IMDb, TMDB, TVDB) and, for an episode, its season and episode numbers. Simkl also receives the title and year, to improve matching.
- Your playback progress, and when playback starts, pauses, and stops
- Titles you watch or mark as watched, with the date, and titles you mark as unwatched

The App only sends this activity and reads your username. It does not import your watch history, ratings, or lists from these services. You can pause sending or exclude libraries in the service's settings in the App.

Data sent to a service is then handled under its own policy, and this Privacy Policy does not cover it:

- [Trakt Privacy Policy](https://trakt.tv/privacy)
- [Simkl Privacy Policy](https://simkl.com/privacy/)

## Data Retention

Strimr retains local data only for as long as you keep the App installed or until you remove the data. You can delete this information by signing out or uninstalling the App.

Trakt and Simkl data stored on your device is kept until you disconnect the service or remove the profile. Updates waiting to be sent are dropped after repeated failures. What a service has already received stays on your account there until you delete it.

## Your Choices

You control all data stored by Strimr. You can:

- Sign out of the App to remove your authentication token
- Reset or change settings at any time
- Turn off error reports in Settings > Privacy to stop sending diagnostics to Sentry
- Disconnect Trakt or Simkl in Settings > Integrations. This removes the tokens, settings, and pending updates for that service from your device, and asks the service to revoke the App's access.
- Request deletion of data already sent to Trakt or Simkl from that service directly. Strimr holds no copy of it and cannot delete it for you.
- Uninstall the App to delete all locally stored data

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
